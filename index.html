How scoring works

Each contact starts at 0. Points are added or subtracted for each rule that applies, and the total is kept between 0 and 100.

RulePoints

What's excluded, and why

Fair Housing law prohibits steering or targeting based on protected classes. Life-event signals can line up with those classes, so this score never uses them as inputs:

  • Divorce, marriage, new baby, death in the family, or aging parents
  • Age, race, colour, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, or disability
  • Neighbourhood demographics, health information, or free-text agent notes

Also by design: only the agent's own contacts are scored (no purchased or scraped lists), agents see a score and a neutral reason rather than raw data, and opted-out contacts are never scored or messaged.
